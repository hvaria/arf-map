This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of the Service. It is

grounds for immediate suspension or termination of your account.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

You may not, and may not permit others to:

- Post false, misleading, or deceptive information about facilities, jobs,
  or job seekers.
- Impersonate any person or entity.
- Use the Service to harass, threaten, defame, or discriminate against any
  individual or group.
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service or any account other
  than your own.
- Scrape, harvest, or otherwise extract data from the Service without
  written permission.
- Use the Service to distribute spam, malware, or unsolicited commercial
  messages.
- Interfere with or disrupt the Service or its infrastructure.
